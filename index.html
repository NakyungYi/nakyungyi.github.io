Man-in-the-Middle Attack & Credential Interception

Built an isolated attack lab on a Microsoft Azure cloud VM, modeling a full network in GNS3 with separate attacker and victim machines connected through a switch and NAT. Ran an ARP spoofing attack to position the attacker between the victim and the gateway, then captured live traffic in Wireshark. Because the target web app ran over HTTP, I intercepted the victim's login request and read the username and password in clear text.

ResultCaptured plaintext credentials off the wire and mapped the impact to the CIA triad, showing exactly why unencrypted traffic breaks confidentiality.

Defense · EncryptINFO 310 · Jan 2026

Securing Web Traffic with TLS / HTTPS

Closed the exact weakness the MITM lab exposed. I generated a private key and certificate signing request with OpenSSL, had the certificate signed by the iSchool root CA, and configured nginx through Docker to serve the application over HTTPS on port 443. I then re-ran the capture in Wireshark to prove the previously readable login traffic was now protected by TLS.

ResultVerified end-to-end encryption and explained which OSI layers stay in clear text after TLS, and why — turning a known vulnerability into a fixed one.
